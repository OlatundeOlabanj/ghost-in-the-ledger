Where do I find my statement?

In your bank app. Open an account, look for Statements or Documents, and download a month as PDF or CSV. A few months gives the best picture.

From alert emails. Open your bank alert emails, select the text, copy it and paste it into the box above. Several at once is fine.

From SMS. Copy the debit and credit messages from your messages app and paste them in.

PDFs are read on your computer only. If a PDF will not open, open it in your browser, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste it here.
